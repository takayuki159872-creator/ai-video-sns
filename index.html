AI-Video-SNS

AI-Video-SNS is a video publishing tool designed to help the account owner prepare and publish short-form video content to authorized social media accounts.

The service is used only with accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner. It does not publish content to accounts without authorization.

Purpose

AI-Video-SNS assists with preparing short-form video content and publishing it through supported social media platform APIs, including TikTok.
