Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

AI-Video-SNS is a tool used to prepare and publish short-form video content to social media accounts authorized by the user.

2. Information We Process

When a user connects a supported social media account, AI-Video-SNS may process information provided through the platform's authorized API, including account identifiers, authorization tokens, profile information, video information, and publishing status.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to authenticate the connected account, prepare content, publish authorized content, prevent duplicate publishing, and monitor the status of publishing operations.

4. Data Sharing

AI-Video-SNS does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to supported social media platforms when required to perform an action authorized by the user.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information processed by the service.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service or satisfy applicable requirements.

7. User Control

Users may revoke access to connected third-party accounts through the applicable platform's account or authorization settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or applicable requirements change.
